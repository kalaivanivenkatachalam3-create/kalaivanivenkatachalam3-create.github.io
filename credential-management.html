Credential Management Automation

A discovery-led initiative to identify and validate high-impact credential workflows so downstream teams could recover more work through payer portals.

Problem

Credential health was a hidden dependency behind portal-based RCM work.

More than 45,000 payer portal credentials supported downstream workflows. Access problems were generating roughly 62,000 tickets each month, and failures could divert work from the lower-cost portal channel to more expensive phone-based follow-up.

My role

This was a discovery-led PM initiative.

I owned problem framing, user interviews, workflow mapping, ticket analysis, automation-fit identification, prioritization, pilot definition and roadmap creation. Engineering owned detailed implementation and technical architecture.

Discovery → prioritization
Quantify

Used access-problem ticket data to size the opportunity rather than relying only on qualitative feedback.

Map workflows

Separated repetitive, deterministic steps from judgment-heavy or upstream/process problems.

Prioritize

Focused on password/username reset, entity mapping and account unlock based on volume and automation fit.

Validate

Used production RPA automations and an in-flight Agentic AI pilot for the more variable entity-mapping workflow.

Outcome & learning

Good automation discovery is also about deciding what not to automate.

The outcome was a focused roadmap around high-impact payer portals and repeatable workflows rather than a blanket automation request. The key PM lesson was to combine user evidence, operational data and engineering feasibility before committing to scale.

Product takeaway

The PM value was not simply finding something to automate — it was using data and workflow discovery to decide what was worth automating, how to validate it, and where to scale.
